impurities in pharmaceuticals to limit potential carcinogenic risk) for mutagenic impurities by considering the synthetic route of
Aripiprazole. The evaluation reveals that the below tabulated mutagenic impurities (Mutagenic impurities from drug substance and
Table 3.2.10 Carryover batch data of mutagenic impurities in drug substance batches) may possible in drug substance, Aripiprazole.

and validated in-line with ICH Q2 [VALIDATION OF ANALYTICAL PROCEDURES: TEXT AND METHODOLOGY]
requirements as part of control strategy to ensure that the level of these impurities in drug substance is below the acceptable limit.
