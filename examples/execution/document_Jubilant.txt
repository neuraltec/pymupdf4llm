**3.2.S.2.2.3    Brief Description of Manufacturing Process** 



7-Hydroxy-3,4-dihydroquinolin-2(1 _H_ )-one (7HDQ) on condensation with 1,4 



Dibromobutane (DBB) gives 7-(4-Bromobutoxy)-3,4-dihydroquinolin-2 (1 _H_ )-one 



(APR-1). 



APR-1 on condensation with 1-(2,3-Dichlorophenyl) piperazine hydrochloride 



(DCPP.HCl) gives Aripiprazole Crude (APR-2), which on purification with 



Isopropyl Alcohol and Purified Water gives Aripiprazole (APR-3).

**3.2.S.2     MANUFACTURE** 



**Blending:** 



Unit operation of blending of individual approved batches has been introduced in 



order to meet the following dispatch situations, 



                  - Requirement for a larger batch size 



                  - To achieve the batch quantity by blending left over product from approved 



batches (as a result of part quantity to dispatch to customers). 



Blending process involves the following steps 



             - Individual approved batches are selected for blending. 



             - Batches are blended, using approved instructions, to achieve the target batch 



size. 



             - Material is tested in accordance against specifications. 



As the blending process is only to upscale the batch size and all the individual 



batches used are approved against the specifications with equivalent quality profile, 



the blending process do not adversely impact the quality of Aripiprazole. 



Note:  The retest period of the blending batches is set on the base of the oldest one.

**CHARACTERISATION** 



**2.** **Inorganic Impurities** 

**a) Residue on Ignition** 

Test for Residue on ignition with the limit of “Not more than 0.1%” is established to have a control on in-organic impurities in- 

line with compendial requirements. 



**b)** **Elemental impurity** 



We confirm that, no class-1, Class-2A, elements as per ICHQ3D are intentionally used in the manufacturing process of 



Aripiprazole USP. 



However, as part of risk evaluation 3 batches of Aripiprazole USP drug substances have been analyzed with validated method by 



ICP-MS for class-1 and class-2A elements and batch result found below detection limit. Batch data and Risk management 



summary and Risk management summary is provided in subsequent pages. 



Analytical method validation report elemental impurities has been provided in section 3.2.S.4.3. 



No other Class-2B and Class-3 elements are intentionally used in the manufacturing process of Aripiprazole Ph.Eur. 



There are no potential contribution of raw material including water, equipment, facility and packaging for carryover of any 



elemental impurities. 



Other element Aluminum (Not classified in ICHQ3D) is used in the manufacturing process of starting material 7HDQ. API batches are 



tested for Aluminum content and results are found at very lower level.
